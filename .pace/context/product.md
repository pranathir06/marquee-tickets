## Vision
Purpose: Simple demo movie ticket booking UI with local-only bookings
Users: Moviegoers browsing showtimes and reserving seats in-browser

## Target Personas
| Persona | Pain Point | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Casual moviegoer | Wants quick view of what’s playing | Pick film and time fast |
| Ticket reserver | Needs seat selection clarity | Choose available seats easily |
| Repeat visitor | Wants to see prior bookings | Review tickets saved in browser |

## MVP Scope
In Scope:
- Browse now showing films and details
- Choose showtime and seats
- Confirm booking and view ticket summary
- View/clear bookings saved in localStorage
Out of Scope:
- Payments or checkout
- Backend APIs or accounts
- Cross-device sync or email tickets

## Strategic Constraints
| Constraint | Reason |
|---|---|
| Local-only storage | README.md: bookings stored in browser only |
| No backend | README.md and AGENTS.md explicitly disallow |
| Fixed routes | AGENTS.md route list must remain |
